A modern, engaging public landing page for a Campus Organization (Aplikasi Organisasi Kampus).

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- **Color Mode**: Light mode by default.
- **Font**: "Inter"
- **Roundness**: `ROUND_EIGHT`
- **Primary Color**: `#1D4ED8` (Academic blue)
- **Secondary Color**: `#F59E0B` (Amber/Gold)
- **Vibe**: Clean, Professional, Accessible, Card-based layouts.

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header/Navigation:** Logo (left), Links (Home, Tentang, Kegiatan, Blog), Login Button (right).
2. **Hero Section:** High-impact headline ("Empowering the Next Generation of Campus Leaders"), a brief subtitle, and a primary call-to-action button ("Join Us" / "Gabung Sekarang"). A placeholder for a hero image or illustration.
3. **Organization Profile (Tentang Kami):** A clean section describing the vision and mission of the organization.
4. **Recent Activities (Kegiatan):** A grid or carousel of 3 recent or upcoming events with placeholder dates and images.
5. **Latest Blog Posts:** A section highlighting 2-3 recent articles from the organization's blog.
6. **Footer:** Quick links, social media icons, copyright, and contact info.
